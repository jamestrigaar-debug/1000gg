Peak Rating vs Rating at the Time

One of the first choices you make in Football DNA Simulator is whether to use Peak Rating or Rating at the Time for your donors. This guide explains the difference and helps you choose.

Quick Comparison

🏆 Peak Rating
  • What it is: Each player's career-best attributes
  • Difficulty: Easier — all donors are strong
  • Realism: Fantasy mode
  • Best for: Beginners, casual play
  • Example: Ronaldo at age 25 (peak form)
📅 Rating at the Time
  • What it is: Attributes from the selected season
  • Difficulty: Harder — players vary by age
  • Realism: Historically accurate
  • Best for: Experienced players, challenge runs
  • Example: Ronaldo at age 35 (declining)

Peak Rating Explained

Peak Rating gives every donor their career-best attributes, regardless of which era you select. This means:

  • Cristiano Ronaldo always has his 2007–2009 attributes (peak form)
  • Thierry Henry always has his 2003–2006 attributes (peak form)
  • Harry Kane always has his 2019–2021 attributes (peak form)

This mode is easier because you're always drafting from the best version of every legend. It's ideal for learning the game or enjoying a more relaxed experience.

Rating at the Time Explained

Rating at the Time uses each player's attributes from the specific season you selected. This means:

  • If you pick the Classic Era (1992–2004), Thierry Henry has his 2003–2004 attributes (young, rising star)
  • If you pick the Recent Era (2015–2024), Thierry Henry has his 2017–2018 attributes (older, declining)
  • If you pick the Current Season (2025–26), Harry Kane has his 2025–26 attributes (in his prime at Tottenham)

This mode is harder because players are weaker in their early and late career years. It's more historically accurate and rewarding for experienced players.

Real-World Example: Cristiano Ronaldo

Peak Rating Mode

Cristiano Ronaldo is always available with his 2007–2009 attributes: 95 Pace, 94 Finishing, 92 Strength. You can draft from him in any era.

Rating at the Time Mode

Classic Era (1992–2004): Ronaldo is not available (he wasn't in the Premier League yet).

Modern Era (2005–2014): Ronaldo has 94 Pace, 93 Finishing, 91 Strength (peak years).

Recent Era (2015–2024): Ronaldo has 89 Pace, 87 Finishing, 88 Strength (older, but still elite).

Current Season (2025–26): Ronaldo may not be available (retired from Premier League).

Which Mode Should You Choose?

Choose Peak Rating if:

  • You're new to Football DNA Simulator
  • You want a more relaxed, casual experience
  • You want to draft from the absolute best version of every legend
  • You're learning the game mechanics

Choose Rating at the Time if:

  • You want a more challenging experience
  • You want historical accuracy (players as they actually were)
  • You enjoy strategic depth (some eras have weaker donors)
  • You've played before and want a new challenge

Strategy Tips

Peak Rating: Focus on finding the best individual donors for each attribute. The game is more forgiving, so you can experiment.

Rating at the Time: Pay attention to player age and career stage. Young players are rising, peak-age players are strongest, and older players are declining. Choose your era strategically.

Can You Change Your Mind?

No — you choose your rating mode at the start of character creation and it applies to your entire career. Choose carefully!
