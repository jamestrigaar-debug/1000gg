Difficulty Guide

Football DNA Simulator offers four difficulty modes that affect your character creation experience. Here's how to choose the right one for you.

The Four Difficulty Modes

🟢 EASY
Rerolls: 3
Attributes: All visible

The most forgiving mode. You get three rerolls during character creation, so you can spin multiple times and pick your favorite build and donors. All attributes are shown, so you know exactly what you're getting.

Best for: Beginners, casual players, learning the game.

🟡 MEDIUM
Rerolls: 1
Attributes: All visible

A balanced challenge. You get one reroll if you don't like your first build, but you need to make a decision quickly. All attributes are visible, so you can plan your DNA draft strategically.

Best for: Players with some experience, balanced challenge.

🔴 HARD
Rerolls: 0
Attributes: All visible

No second chances. You get one build roll and must live with it. However, all attributes are visible, so you can see exactly what you're working with and plan your DNA draft accordingly.

Best for: Experienced players, players who enjoy commitment.

💀 IMPOSSIBLE
Rerolls: 0
Attributes: Names only

The ultimate challenge. You get one build roll with no rerolls, and you only see player names when drafting DNA — no attributes. You must draft blind, relying on your knowledge of real Premier League players.

Best for: Hardcore players, football experts, challenge runs.

Important: Difficulty Only Affects Creation

Your difficulty choice only matters during character creation (the build roll and DNA drafting). Once your career starts, all difficulties play identically. The game engine, match simulation, and career progression are the same regardless of difficulty.

In other words: Easy and Impossible careers have the same career simulation — the only difference is how much help you get when building your player.

Choosing Your Difficulty

Choose Easy if:

  • You're new to Football DNA Simulator
  • You want to experiment with different DNA combinations
  • You want to focus on the career simulation, not the draft
  • You want to learn the game without pressure

Choose Medium if:

  • You've played before and want a bit more challenge
  • You like having one safety net (one reroll)
  • You want to balance strategy with some randomness

Choose Hard if:

  • You're experienced and want commitment
  • You like the pressure of one-shot decisions
  • You want to see all attributes and plan strategically
  • You enjoy speedrunning or challenge runs

Choose Impossible if:

  • You're a football expert and know Premier League players well
  • You want the ultimate challenge
  • You enjoy drafting blind based on player knowledge
  • You're doing a challenge run or streaming

Strategy Tips by Difficulty

Easy Mode Strategy

Use your rerolls to find a build you like. Don't rush — you have flexibility. Once you have a good build, focus on finding donors with complementary attributes (e.g., if your build is tall and strong, draft for finishing and speed).

Medium Mode Strategy

Your one reroll is precious. If your first build is acceptable, consider keeping it and moving forward. If it's poor (very short, very weak), use your reroll. Once you commit, focus on finding the best donors for your build.

Hard Mode Strategy

You must commit to your first build. Accept it and move forward. Since you can see all attributes, plan your DNA draft carefully to complement your build. For example, if you're short, prioritize speed and agility donors.

Impossible Mode Strategy

You're drafting blind. Rely on your knowledge of real Premier League players. Think about their playing styles, positions, and strengths. For example:

  • Know that Thierry Henry was fast and had great finishing
  • Know that Rio Ferdinand was strong and had great positioning
  • Know that David Beckham had great crossing and set pieces

Your football knowledge is your advantage.

Can You Change Difficulty?

No — you choose your difficulty at the start and it applies to your entire career. Choose wisely!
