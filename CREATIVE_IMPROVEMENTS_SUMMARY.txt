FEATURE DETAILS
================================================================================

1. EVENT DIFFICULTY SCALING

Purpose: Adapt event difficulty to player performance

Performance Multipliers:
  - Sensational: 1.3x (30% harder, 30% bigger rewards)
  - Overperformed: 1.15x (15% harder, 15% bigger rewards)
  - Met Expectation: 1.0x (normal)
  - Underperformed: 0.85x (15% easier, 15% smaller penalties)
  - Flop: 0.7x (30% easier, 30% smaller penalties)

Example:
  Normal player choosing "Demand more playing time":
    - rep -2, Ambition +8, Ego +4
  Sensational player:
    - rep -3, Ambition +10, Ego +5 (30% harder)
  Flopping player:
    - rep -1, Ambition +5, Ego +2 (30% easier)

Benefits:
  ✅ Dynamic challenge that adapts to performance
  ✅ Struggling players get help, elite players get tested
  ✅ Events feel more appropriate to circumstances
  ✅ Replayability: same events feel different at different levels

2. CAREER MOMENTUM TRACKING

Purpose: Reward consistent decision-making patterns

Streak Types:
  - Ambitious: High Ambition pillar changes
  - Loyal: High Loyalty pillar changes
  - Professional: High Professionalism pillar changes
  - Adaptable: High Adaptability pillar changes
  - Leader: High Leadership pillar changes

Bonuses:
  - At 3 consecutive similar choices: +1 rep, +4 Consistency
  - At 5 consecutive similar choices: +3 rep, +8 Consistency

Mechanics:
  1. Player makes a choice with specific pillar focus
  2. Momentum tracker records choice type
  3. If next choice has same focus, streak continues
  4. Streak length increases, momentum score increases
  5. At milestones (3, 5), bonuses are applied

Benefits:
  ✅ Narrative arc: decisions build toward archetypes
  ✅ Reward consistency: committed playstyles get bonuses
  ✅ Emergent storytelling: streaks create story beats
  ✅ Replayability: different streaks = different careers

3. PILLAR SYNERGY SYSTEM

Purpose: Create emergent character archetypes

Synergy Archetypes:
  
  Visionary Leader:
    - Requires: Ambition ≥ 60, Leadership ≥ 60
    - Bonuses: +2 rep, +1 fame
    - Description: Natural leader who inspires others
  
  Reliable Professional:
    - Requires: Loyalty ≥ 60, Professionalism ≥ 60
    - Bonuses: +1 contract year, +1 rep
    - Description: One-club man who commands respect
  
  Versatile Player:
    - Requires: Adaptability ≥ 60, Consistency ≥ 60
    - Bonuses: +1 attribute point
    - Description: Can play multiple positions effectively
  
  Relentless Competitor:
    - Requires: KillerInstinct ≥ 60, Durability ≥ 60
    - Bonuses: +2 goals, +1 rep
    - Description: Scores despite physical demands
  
  Natural Captain:
    - Requires: Leadership ≥ 65, Loyalty ≥ 65
    - Bonuses: +1 intl cap, +2 rep
    - Description: International captain material

Benefits:
  ✅ Emergent archetypes: pillar combinations create types
  ✅ Playstyle rewards: different builds have strengths
  ✅ Strategic depth: balance pillars strategically
  ✅ Replayability: different synergies = different careers

4. EVENT CONSEQUENCE CHAINS

Purpose: Create ripple effects from decisions

How It Works:
  1. Player makes a choice
  2. Momentum tracker records the choice
  3. Streak is updated (continues or resets)
  4. Momentum bonuses are calculated
  5. Synergies are recalculated
  6. Synergy bonuses are applied
  7. All effects cascade together

Example Chain:
  - Player chooses "Demand more playing time" (Ambition +8)
  - Momentum tracker records "ambitious" choice
  - Streak continues (3rd ambitious choice)
  - Momentum bonus: +1 rep, +4 Consistency
  - Synergies recalculated: Visionary now active
  - Synergy bonus: +2 rep, +1 fame
  - Total effect: rep +11, Ambition +8, Consistency +4, Fame +1

Benefits:
  ✅ Decisions have meaningful consequences
  ✅ Cascading effects feel organic
  ✅ Emergent gameplay from simple rules
  ✅ Replayability: different chains = different outcomes

5. PILLAR SYNERGY BONUSES

Purpose: Reward strategic pillar building

Active Synergies:
  - Visionary: +2 rep, +1 fame
  - Reliable: +1 contract, +1 rep
  - Versatile: +1 attribute bonus
  - Relentless: +2 goals, +1 rep
  - Captain: +1 intl cap, +2 rep

How Bonuses Apply:
  1. At end of season, calculate all synergies
  2. For each active synergy, apply bonuses
  3. Multiple synergies stack
  4. Bonuses are applied automatically

Example:
  Player with:
    - Ambition 65, Leadership 65 (Visionary)
    - KillerInstinct 70, Durability 70 (Relentless)
  
  Bonuses applied:
    - Visionary: +2 rep, +1 fame
    - Relentless: +2 goals, +1 rep
    - Total: +3 rep, +1 fame, +2 goals

Benefits:
  ✅ Reward strategic pillar building
  ✅ Multiple synergies stack for big effects
  ✅ Encourages diverse pillar development
  ✅ Creates build diversity
