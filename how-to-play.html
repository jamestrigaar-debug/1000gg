How to Play Football DNA Simulator

Football DNA Simulator is a free browser-based football career simulator where you draft attributes from real Premier League legends and simulate a striker's career chasing 1000 goals. Here's how it works.

The 6-Step Gameplay Loop

1Pick Your Era and Rating Mode

Start by choosing which era of Premier League football you want to play in:

  • All Eras (1992–2026) — Mix players from any season
  • Classic Era (1992–2004) — The golden age of Cantona, Shearer, Bergkamp
  • Modern Era (2005–2014) — Ronaldo, Messi, Rooney, Van Persie
  • Recent Era (2015–2024) — Kane, Salah, De Bruyne, Son
  • Current Season (2025–26) — Today's Premier League squad

Then choose your rating mode:

  • Peak Rating — Donors always use their career-best attributes
  • Rating at the Time — Attributes reflect their performance in the selected season
2Select Your Difficulty

Choose how challenging you want your career to be:

  • Easy — 3 rerolls, all attributes visible
  • Medium — 1 reroll, all attributes visible
  • Hard — 0 rerolls, all attributes visible
  • Impossible — 0 rerolls, names only (no attributes shown)

Difficulty only affects character creation. Once your career starts, all difficulties play identically.

3Roll Your Build

Your country of origin, academy tier, and physical build (height and weight) are randomly rolled. These affect:

  • Your starting attributes and potential
  • Your initial club and contract terms
  • Your international eligibility

You cannot change these rolls (except on Easy/Medium difficulty, where you get rerolls).

4Draft DNA from Premier League Legends

This is the core mechanic. You spin for 5 random Premier League players and choose which attribute to inherit from each:

  • Heading Ability — Aerial threat, headers, crosses
  • Body Strength — Physical presence, shielding, collisions
  • Left Foot — Weaker-foot finishing and passing
  • Right Foot — Dominant-foot finishing and passing
  • Speed & Mentality — Pace, determination, composure

Each donor's other attributes also influence your player through hidden-influence blending, so the combination of donors matters as much as individual picks.

5Simulate Your Career

Your player starts at age 17 in their academy. Each season you:

  • Play matches and accumulate goals
  • Make transfer decisions (stay or move clubs)
  • Negotiate contracts (wages, length, bonuses)
  • Manage injuries and recovery
  • Pursue international caps for England
  • Chase trophies: league titles, domestic cups, European competitions

Your decisions affect your career trajectory, injury risk, and final legacy.

6Chase 1000 Career Goals

The ultimate goal is to score 1000 career goals before retirement (typically age 35–38). Along the way, you can pursue:

  • Premier League titles
  • Domestic cups (FA Cup, League Cup)
  • European trophies (Champions League, Europa League)
  • International tournaments (World Cup, Euros)
  • Individual awards (Ballon d'Or, Golden Boot)

Key Mechanics Explained

Peak Rating vs Rating at the Time

Peak Rating uses each player's career-best attributes, regardless of season. This makes it easier to find strong donors and is better for beginners.

Rating at the Time uses attributes from the specific season you selected. A player might be weaker in their early career or decline in their later years. This is more challenging and historically accurate.

Hidden-Influence Blending

When you draft DNA, you explicitly pick one attribute from each donor. But the other donors' attributes also subtly influence your player through hidden blending. This means the combination of donors matters, not just individual picks.

Derived Stats

Some attributes are derived from your DNA picks:

  • Agility & Balance — Derived from Speed and Body Strength
  • Dribbling & Finishing — Derived from Foot attributes and Mentality
  • Longevity — How long your player stays fit and competitive
  • Injury Rating — Susceptibility to injuries

Transfers and Contracts

Each season, you can accept or reject transfer offers. Better clubs offer higher wages and more playing time, but also higher expectations. Refusing too many offers can damage your reputation.

International Duty

Your player can earn caps for England in World Cups (every 4 seasons) and European Championships (every 2 seasons). International tournaments can boost your reputation and goal tally.

Tips for Reaching 1000 Goals

  • Draft for finishing — Prioritize Right Foot and Mentality to maximize goal-scoring potential
  • Play for attacking teams — Clubs with strong attacks create more goal-scoring chances
  • Avoid injuries — Manage your training and recovery to stay fit
  • Stay at your peak — Transfer to ambitious clubs while you're still young and hungry
  • Play longer — Longevity is key; careers that last into the late 30s have more time to accumulate goals
