How to play
  1. Draft your manager. Five rolls — tactic, origin, career, and two separate draws from football's coaching history. What they add up to is never shown; you find out from the job offers.
  2. Take a job. Read the boardroom style before you sign. An Aggressive board sacks you in a year.
  3. Pre-season decisions. Two cards that shape the season — sign a striker, drill the defence, promise the board the world.
  4. Simulate the season. Every division in the world plays out at once.
  5. Face the board. They score you on league finish, cup run, finances and youth, weighted by their style.
  6. End-of-season decisions. React to what happened, then go again — until you are sacked.
