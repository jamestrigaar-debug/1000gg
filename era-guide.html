Era Guide

Football DNA Simulator lets you draft from five different eras of Premier League football, each with its own legendary players and playing style. Here's what you need to know about each era.

The Five Eras

🏆 All Eras (1992–2026)
All Premier League seasons combined

The ultimate mixed bag. You can draft from any Premier League player across all 34 seasons. This gives you the most flexibility and access to the broadest range of playing styles and eras.

Best for: Players who want maximum choice and variety. You can mix legends from different eras in a single career.

Available legends: Cantona, Shearer, Bergkamp, Zidane, Ronaldo, Ronaldinho, Messi, Ronaldo (CR7), Salah, Kane, De Bruyne, and hundreds more across 34 seasons.
👑 Classic Era (1992–2004)
The golden age of English football

The Premier League's first 13 seasons. This era is defined by physical, direct football and iconic English strikers. The pace is slower, the defending is harder, and the style is more traditional.

Playing style: Physical, direct, high-intensity. Fewer technical players, more traditional wingers and strikers.

Available legends: Eric Cantona, Alan Shearer, Ian Wright, Teddy Sheringham, Thierry Henry (early), Patrick Vieira, Roy Keane, Peter Schmeichel, and many more.

Best for: Players who love the classic era and want to experience football from the 1990s and early 2000s.

⚡ Modern Era (2005–2014)
The era of superstars

The rise of global superstars and attacking football. This era saw the dominance of Cristiano Ronaldo, Thierry Henry, and the emergence of technical, skillful players. The pace increased and the football became more entertaining.

Playing style: Technical, attacking, fast-paced. More emphasis on individual skill and dribbling.

Available legends: Cristiano Ronaldo (peak), Thierry Henry (peak), Wayne Rooney, Robin van Persie, Didier Drogba, Sergio Agüero (early), and many more.

Best for: Players who love the superstar era and want to draft from the best attacking players of the 2000s and early 2010s.

🌟 Recent Era (2015–2024)
The modern Premier League

The era of tactical sophistication and global talent. This period saw the rise of data-driven football, pressing systems, and the influx of world-class players from around the globe. The pace is high, the defending is intelligent, and the football is technically excellent.

Playing style: Tactical, pressing-based, high-intensity. Emphasis on movement, positioning, and team coordination.

Available legends: Mohamed Salah, Harry Kane, Kevin De Bruyne, Sergio Agüero (late), Virgil van Dijk, Son Heung-min, and many more.

Best for: Players who want to experience modern Premier League football with contemporary tactics and players.

🔥 Current Season (2025–26)
Today's Premier League

The latest Premier League season. This is the most up-to-date era with the current squad of every club. Perfect for players who want to draft from today's stars.

Playing style: Modern, high-intensity, tactical. The latest evolution of Premier League football.

Available legends: The current 2025–26 Premier League squad, including the latest signings and rising stars.

Best for: Players who want to play with today's Premier League stars and see how they perform in a career simulation.

Era Strategy Tips

Classic Era (1992–2004)

This era is physically demanding. Prioritize strength and heading ability. The game is more direct, so pace and power matter more than technical skill. Look for traditional strikers like Shearer and Wright.

Modern Era (2005–2014)

This is the era of superstars. You have access to Ronaldo and Henry at their peak. Prioritize finishing and pace. Technical skill is highly rewarded. This era is generally easier because the donors are so strong.

Recent Era (2015–2024)

This era rewards tactical intelligence and movement. Prioritize positioning and composure. The game is faster and more pressing-based. Look for modern strikers like Salah and Kane who excel in high-intensity systems.

Current Season (2025–26)

The most up-to-date era. Expect modern tactics and contemporary players. This is ideal if you want to play with today's Premier League stars.

All Eras (1992–2026)

Maximum flexibility. You can mix and match from any era. This is ideal for players who want to experiment and aren't committed to a specific era's playing style.

Which Era Should You Choose?

  • For nostalgia: Classic Era (1992–2004)
  • For superstars: Modern Era (2005–2014)
  • For contemporary football: Recent Era (2015–2024) or Current Season (2025–26)
  • For maximum choice: All Eras (1992–2026)
